Official Mid-Sem Exam Blueprint • 14 Oct 2026

Syllabus: L01 – L14 (All Lectures) Weightage: 20% Ref Sheet Provided
Exam Section Structure
  • Sec A (35 M, 40 Min limit): 20 MCQs, 3 Integer, 3 Match (3M each).
  • Sec B (65 Marks): 4 Coding (2 Verilog HDL + 2 MIPS Assembly).
  • Sec C: 3 Subjective architectural analysis questions.
  • Sec D (Written Paper): 2 Circuit schematics & equations (Half/Full Adder, logic gates, instruction encoding/decoding, J/R/I types).
Must-Practice MIPS Programs & Circuits
  • MIPS Programs: Even/Odd check, sum of N natural numbers, sum of odd/even with limits, sum of array elements, Factorial, Fibonacci series.
  • Hardware Blocks: MUX, DEMUX, Half/Full Adder, Counter, Register, 3-to-8 Decoder (8 outputs).
  • Lab & Bonus: Review previous & current labs; Bonus on logic gate circuit architecture & debugging.
